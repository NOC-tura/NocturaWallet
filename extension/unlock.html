Three layers protect your wallet

You hold the keys. We never can.

Password

At least 12 characters you choose. It unlocks the wallet in this browser and is asked again before sends to new addresses or large amounts.

Passkey (optional)

A fingerprint, face or security key as a shortcut. Your password still works for everything.

Recovery seed

24 words. Written offline. The only way back if this browser's data is lost.

If you lose all three, no one — not Noctura, not a Solana validator — can recover your funds. That's the point.
